Physical AI robots send RGB images and LiDAR point clouds over wireless links. Our system learns their shared context and transmits only the information needed for reconstruction.

Built with **SK Telecom**, the prototype combines Unitree Go2 data collection with a Python OFDM/SDR link for over-the-air testing.
