*Entanglement* is a site-native immersive installation created with **Artificial Nature (Haru Ji + Graham Wakefield)** for the XR Lab at Ulsan Art Museum. Yonsei University’s Intelligence Networking Lab contributed to the work through Chan-Byoung Chae and Dongha Choi.
